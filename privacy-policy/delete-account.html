Account Deletion Request for SmartFarm

At SmartFarm, developed by IoT Innovators, we value your control over your data. You have the right to request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated information in accordance with Google Play's Data Safety policies.

Steps to Request Account Deletion

Please follow these steps to permanently delete your account and associated data:

Step 1: Choose Your Method

  • Method A: In-App Request (Fastest)
    Open the SmartFarm app, navigate to the Account section, and select Delete Account. Follow the on-screen prompts to confirm.
  • Method B: Email Request
    Send an email to smartfarm-ceng322@googlegroups.com from the email address registered with your account.

Step 2: Provide Information

If requesting via email, please include the following in your message:

  • Subject: "Account Deletion Request - SmartFarm"
  • Your Full Name (exactly as it appears in your profile)
  • The email address associated with your SmartFarm account

Step 3: Verification

For your security, we will send a confirmation email to your registered address. You must reply to this email to verify that you are the owner of the account before we proceed with the deletion.

Types of Data to be Deleted

Upon successful verification, the following data types will be permanently removed from our servers:

  • Personal Identifiers: Name, Email Address, Phone Number.
  • Authentication Records: Firebase UID and login credentials.
  • Environmental History: All sensor logs (Temperature, Humidity, Soil Moisture, Light) historically linked to your account.
  • Alert & Notification History: Records of all past alerts sent to your device.
  • Hardware Links: The association between your user account and the MAC addresses of your ESP32 or Raspberry Pi devices.

Data Retention Policy

  • Retention Periods: Once you confirm the deletion, your data is immediately removed from our live "Production" database. It may persist in our encrypted database backups for up to 30 days, after which it is permanently overwritten.
  • Retained Data: We do not retain any identifiable personal data. We may retain fully anonymized environmental data (data that can no longer be linked to you or your specific device) for the purpose of improving our farm monitoring algorithms.
  • Legal Requirements: We may retain specific transaction records if required by law for financial or regulatory compliance.
